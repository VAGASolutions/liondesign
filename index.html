Expert tips

Quick wins for your website

Implement these today and see results within weeks

SEO

Optimize your title tags

Keep titles between 50–60 characters, put the primary keyword near the start, and make every page title unique.

Impact:
High
Security

Add a Content Security Policy

A CSP header dramatically reduces XSS risk by whitelisting trusted sources for scripts, styles, and media.

Impact:
Very High
Performance

Convert images to WebP

WebP is 25–35% smaller than JPEG/PNG at the same quality. Use <picture> for progressive enhancement.

Impact:
High
Reach

Submit an XML sitemap

A valid sitemap.xml tells search engines exactly what to index. Submit it via Google Search Console immediately.

Impact:
Critical
SEO

Fix broken internal links

Broken links (404s) waste crawl budget and frustrate users. Audit monthly and redirect or fix every broken URL.

Impact:
High
Security

Enable HSTS

HTTP Strict Transport Security forces HTTPS for all future visits, preventing protocol downgrade attacks.

Impact:
Very High
